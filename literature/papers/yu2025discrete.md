# Discrete Diffusion in LLMs and Multimodal Models: A Survey

## TL;DR

Systematic survey of discrete diffusion LMs (dLLMs) and discrete diffusion multimodal LMs (dMLLMs); historical development, mathematical formalism, common modeling methods, representative model catalog, training/inference techniques, quantization, and trustworthiness.

## Claims (as a survey)

- dLLMs/dMLLMs use full-attention multi-token parallel decoding; this naturally enables fine-grained output control and dynamic perception in ways AR cannot.
- A growing number of industrial-scale and open-source dLLMs match AR performance with up to 10× inference speedup.
